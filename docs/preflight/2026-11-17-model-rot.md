# Design brief: model-rot

> Date: 2026-11-17 · Tier: 1 · Status: built (MVP)

**Evidence:** the official deprecation pages of Anthropic, OpenAI and Google (fetched 2026-11-17; retired IDs fail hard; Gemini silently aliases some). Open issues with stale defaults: OpenMetadata#36757 (2026-10-31), IBM/mcp-context-forge#7375 (2026-10-29), Flowise#6698 (Assistants API).

**Prior art:** llm-model-deprecation (0★; hand-curated registry, **stale on the day checked**: gpt-3.5-turbo and gpt-5 listed as active), depdesk (0★, OpenAI and Anthropic), flake8-llm-sunset, sunsetscan (printemps-tokyo, 0★). LiteLLM's model map has deprecation dates but no scanner. **Differentiator:** live parsing of the primary pages, a 4-state output including silent aliasing, and Bedrock/Vertex forms.

**Fixed during the build:** IDs listed inside parentheses were dropped (a rule meant for Gemini's prose notes); active models with "not sooner than" dates were reported as retiring; display labels ("GPT-4") matched case-insensitively.

**Verification:** 4 offline tests on fixtures of all three page formats; live lookups covering all four states; 2 real repos with open issues reproduced.
